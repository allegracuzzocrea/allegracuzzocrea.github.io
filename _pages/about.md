I am a PhD student working at the intersection of Computational Intelligence and Quantum Computing.

My research focuses on developing innovative computational methodologies to address some of the key challenges that limit the efficiency and reliability of quantum computation.
In particular, my work explores reinforcement learning, evolutionary computation, fuzzy systems, and machine learning as tools for developing intelligent and adaptive solutions to quantum computing challenges, including quantum circuit transpilation and synthesis, quantum hardware selection, quantum error correction, and quantum error mitigation.

## Research Interests

- Quantum Computing
- Computational Intelligence
- Quantum Circuit Transpilation and Synthesis
- Reinforcement Learning
- Evolutionary Computation
- Quantum Error Mitigation
- Quantum Error Correction
